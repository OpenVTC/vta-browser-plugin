VTA Wallet — reviewer demo

Sign in to authorise your wallet against the demo agent, and for the walkthrough of what to test.

1. First time here

Use the reviewer key ID from this listing's test instructions. This is how you get in before the wallet is set up — you will need it to authorise your wallet in the first place.

2. Once your wallet is set up

Sign in with the wallet itself, over SIOPv2. This is the flow under review: the wallet proves control of your DID to the demo agent, and this site checks the result with the agent rather than taking the browser's word for it. It works only after you have completed step 1 and onboarded.

The wallet is not available on this page yet. Open the extension's popup and choose Enable on this site — until you do, the wallet is invisible here by design. The page reloads once you enable it.
